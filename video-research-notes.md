## In-course link check

The public HRIS Foundation lesson was opened without authentication. Its video briefing card exposed the expected title, source, recommended status, outcome, and a “Watch external video” action resolving to the validated AIHR HRIS URL. The same card also showed the 45-minute guided practical session, three numbered steps, and the stated deliverable.

## Selection approach

The course will link to external public videos rather than embed or reproduce them. Recommendations will be labelled as supplementary context, not formal certification or tenant-access training. The learner will always receive a guided, self-directed practical session immediately after the video so that viewing remains active rather than passive.
